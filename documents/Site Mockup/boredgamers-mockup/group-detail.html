Portland Board Gamers

12 members Public

A group for board game enthusiasts in the Portland area. We meet weekly for game nights and welcome players of all skill levels.

About This Group

Portland Board Gamers is a welcoming community for tabletop enthusiasts of all experience levels. Whether you're a seasoned strategist or just learning your first game, you'll find a place here.

We meet every Saturday evening at rotating locations around the Portland metro area. Most game nights run from 6 PM to 10 PM, but feel free to come and go as your schedule allows.

Group Rules:
1. Be respectful to all players
2. Teach games patiently to new players
3. Clean up after yourself
4. Have fun!
