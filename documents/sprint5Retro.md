## Summary

#### AI Tools in Development Workflow

The team discussed their experiences using AI tools in their development work, with Adler sharing his initial resistance but now accepting AI as necessary for efficiency. They acknowledged that while AI can help identify bugs and reduce development time, there are still learning curves and some anxiety around working with AI in command line environments. Logan suggested that AI allows for faster iteration on features without early optimization, comparing it to the gaming development principle of not optimizing before having a complete working version.

### Sprint Challenges with Race Conditions

The team discussed challenges with race conditions and BDD tests during the recent sprint. Ian emphasized the need for humans to be more specific with AI instructions, as AI will only follow exact directions without considering potential issues like race conditions. The team also noted that BDD tests required significant JavaScript implementation, which they hadn't anticipated initially. While the sprint was completed ahead of schedule with 8 user points achieved, Logan expressed some disappointment with their individual progress due to various distractions.

### Sprint Progress Review Meeting

The team discussed their progress in the current sprint, with Adler noting that they completed user stories early and spent additional time on bug fixes and cleanup work. Logan confirmed he also focused on extra tasks beyond the planned user stories. The team expressed satisfaction with their progress and agreed to meet again on Sunday.
