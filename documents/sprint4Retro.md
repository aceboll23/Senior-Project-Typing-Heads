# Sprint 4 Retrospective

## Quick Recap

The team conducted a Sprint 4 retrospective, focusing primarily on the challenges they faced with BDD (Behavior-Driven Development) testing. Ian, Logan, and Adler discussed how there was a miscommunication about BDD testing requirements, particularly regarding whether to use a real database or mock data, which caused significant difficulties and nearly led to a missed deadline.
